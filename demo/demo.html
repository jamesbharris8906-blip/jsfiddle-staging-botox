You will hear what the provider sees, what they would do first, what it may cost, and how long the change may last, and then you can decide in your own time.

Botox

Think of Botox as a pause button for the muscles that crease your skin. When those muscles ease off, the forehead, the space between the brows, and the corners of the eyes all look calmer. Your provider chooses a small dose for each spot, and the whole thing is done in minutes. A faint pink mark may linger for a short while, then fade. Over the next week or two the lines soften, and the effect usually stays for three to four months.

Lip Filler

With lip filler, the aim is shape first and size second. Do you want the top lip to match the bottom one? A crisper border? A little more fullness when you smile? Say so, and your provider will place a soft gel in tiny amounts until the lips look even and natural. A numbing step comes first, and the lips may puff up for a day or two before they settle into their new shape.

Facial Balancing

Instead of fixing one spot and hoping the rest keeps up, facial balancing steps back and looks at how all the parts work together. Cheeks, chin, jaw, and lips are checked side by side, and filler goes where a small change will make the biggest difference. The provider adds a little, checks the mirror with you, and adds more only if it helps. The end point is a face that looks like you, just more rested and more even.

Microneedling

Microneedling works by waking up your skin, not by covering it up. Fine needles make tiny, brief openings, and your body responds by building fresh collagen in the weeks that follow. That fresh collagen can smooth fine lines, soften rough patches, shrink the look of pores, and fade old spot marks. Numbing cream goes on first, and the pink look afterward is much like a mild sunburn that fades in a day or two.

Cheek Filler

When cheeks lose their lift, the whole face can look drawn down. Cheek filler goes deep, close to the bone, to give that lift back and to ease the folds that run toward the mouth. Both sides are matched by eye, from the front and in profile, so nothing looks lopsided. The lift is there as soon as you sit up, and it can hold for many months before a small top-up is needed.

Diamond Glow Facial

A Diamond Glow Facial does three jobs in one sitting: it clears away dull surface skin, it draws out what is sitting in the pores, and it presses a serum into the fresh skin below. The diamond tip feels cool, the suction is light, and most people leave with skin that looks brighter at once. It suits a day before photos or a night out, because there is no real downtime to plan around.

Xeomin

Xeomin does the same kind of job as Botox, easing the muscles that make lines, but its mix is simpler, with no added proteins. That plain mix suits people who like a clean option, and it suits people who want to try something new after Botox. The forehead, the brow lines, and the eye corners are the usual spots. Expect a short visit, a change that grows over a few days, and a soft look for about three months.

Microneedling With Exosomes

Here the needles do their usual work, and then exosomes join in. Those tiny messengers help skin cells pass signals as they heal, which can speed up collagen building and help the pink fade sooner. You may still look flushed for a day or two, but the recovery often feels gentler. It suits guests who want a stronger microneedling visit, or whose skin has healed slowly in the past.

Hair Restoration Microneedling

Thin hair often starts at the scalp, so that is where this treatment goes. Fine needles stir up the skin around the roots and bring more blood flow to the area, which can help hair grow back looking thicker. It takes a run of visits to see a real change, and each visit is short enough to fit into a normal day. If your part has grown wider or your crown feels thin, bring it up at your visit.

Chin Filler

A chin that is short or soft can throw off the whole side view, even when the rest of the face looks fine. Chin filler uses a firm gel to add length and shape, so the lips, chin, and jaw sit in a better line. The gel goes in a little at a time, with a check from each side, and the change is there right away. A little puffiness is normal, and it settles in the days that follow.

Jawline Filler

For a jaw that has lost its edge, filler can draw the line back in. Placed along the bone in small amounts, it firms the lower face and softens the look of jowls, all without surgery. Your provider will look at the shape from the front and from the side before they finish. In photos the jaw looks sharper, and in person it still looks soft and natural. The shape can hold for many months.

SkinMedica Chemical Peels Illuminize

Illuminize is the lightest of the three SkinMedica peels, and it is made for a quick glow. It sweeps away dull surface cells, so the skin underneath looks brighter and smoother within a few days. There is almost no visible peeling, which makes it a good first peel and a good choice before a big event. Your provider will show you how to care for your skin that night, and most guests book it again when the glow fades.

SkinMedica Chemical Peels Vitalize

Vitalize sits in the middle: stronger than Illuminize, gentler than Rejuvanize. It works on fine lines, rough spots, and tone that looks uneven, and you may see light flaking for a few days as old skin gives way to new. The visit itself is short, and you will leave with clear steps for washing and for what to skip. With a few peels spaced apart, the change can build into something that lasts.

SkinMedica Chemical Peels Rejuvanize

Rejuvanize is the strongest of the three peels, for skin with deeper lines, sun marks, and rough texture. Expect more peeling over several days, so give yourself a quiet week and follow the aftercare steps closely. Your provider will check your skin first, because this peel is not right for all skin types. When the new skin comes through, it can look smoother, brighter, and more even than before.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does Botox feel like?

It feels like a few quick pinches, and then it is done. The needles are fine, the provider moves fast, and most people are surprised at how short the visit is. A small red mark may show for a while, then fade. If you are nervous, say so at the start, and your provider will explain each step before they do it.

How long will I wait to see it work?

Give it a few days to start, and one to two weeks to show its full effect. The slow build is a plus, because the change comes in gently and looks natural. A photo from the day of your visit, next to one from two weeks later, will show the change better than a daily look in the mirror.

How long does the soft look stay?

Most people keep the soft look for three to four months. The spot, the dose, and how fast your body clears it can shift that time a little. Some guests return at three months, and others stretch it out a bit longer. Your provider can help you set a return time that fits how your face looks and feels.

How much will my visit cost?

That depends on the spots you treat and the amount your plan needs, so there is no single price for all. Before any work starts, your provider will give you a clear price, so you can decide with no surprise. Call (614) 412-4487 if you want a sense of what a plan for your goals may cost.

Can I have Botox and a facial on the same day?

Sometimes yes, and sometimes it is better to space them out. Your provider will look at your skin and your plan, and tell you what order makes sense. A facial can be a good pick before Botox, or a few days after, when the tiny spots have settled. Ask at your visit, and they will map out the order with you.

What should I avoid after Botox?

For the rest of the day, avoid hard workouts, hot baths, saunas, and pressing or rubbing the treated spots. Try not to lie face down for a long time. You can wash your face with a gentle touch, and you can wear light makeup after a few hours. If you are unsure about anything the next day, call the spa and ask.

Am I too young, or too old, for Botox?

There is no one right age. Some people start in their late twenties to soften early lines, and some start much later to soften lines that have been there for years. What matters is your skin, your muscles, and your goals, not your age on its own. Your provider will tell you honestly if Botox fits, or if another treatment would suit you better.
