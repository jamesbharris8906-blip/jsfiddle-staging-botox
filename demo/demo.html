Five Things I Wish I Had Known Before My First Visit

First, the visit is short. A consultation and a treatment can often fit into a lunch break, and most people go back to their day right after. You do not need to clear a whole afternoon, and you do not need to plan a long recovery for most treatments.

Second, a pinch is not a big pain. The needle used for Botox is fine, and the pinch passes fast. Fillers and microneedling feel different, but your provider will tell you what to expect before they start, so there are no shocks.

Third, the change is slow. Botox builds over days, microneedling builds over weeks, and fillers settle in the first days. That slow change is a good thing, because it looks natural and it gives you time to get used to it.

Fourth, you are in charge. You can say yes to one treatment and no to another. You can ask for a light change, you can take the plan home, and you can come back later. A good provider will not push you to decide on the spot.

Fifth, aftercare is simple. Keep the skin clean and calm, skip harsh products for a short time, and shield it from strong sun. Your provider will give you clear steps, and you can call if a question comes up later.

Xeomin

Xeomin is an FDA-approved injectable that is close to Botox in how it works. It eases the small muscles that make lines when you frown, squint, or smile, so those lines look softer. It is a good choice for people who want a natural look and a short visit, and for people who have tried Botox and want to talk about another option. Your provider can help you weigh the two at your free consultation.

Botox

Botox is the injectable most people know by name, and it is FDA-approved. A fine needle puts a small amount into the muscles that make forehead lines, frown lines, and crow's feet. The muscle eases for a while, and the skin above it looks smoother. You can still smile and raise your brows, because the goal is a fresh look, not a flat one. The visit is short, and the change builds over a few days.

Facial Balancing

Facial balancing is a plan for the whole face at once. The provider looks at the chin, cheeks, lips, and jaw together, and then uses small amounts of filler and injectables in a few spots to bring them into line. A small lift in the cheek can help the jaw, and a small change to the chin can help the lips. The end look is even and soft, and it should still look like you.

Lip Filler

Lip filler uses a dermal filler to add shape and soft volume to the lips. It can help lips that are thin, uneven, or less full than they used to be. Your provider will talk about the shape and size that fits your face, and will place small amounts with care. You can start light and add more later, and the lip should look soft and natural.

Cheek Filler

Cheek filler adds volume back to the middle of the face, where time can leave cheeks flat or tired. A small amount can lift the cheek, shape the cheekbone, and make the face look brighter. It can also help the skin under the eye look less hollow. Your provider will check your smile and your face at rest, then place the filler where it gives the best lift.

Chin Filler

Chin filler uses a dermal filler to shape the chin and give it more shape and balance. A chin that sits back a little can make the lips or nose look out of line, and a small change there can bring the whole profile into shape. The provider will look at your profile from the side as well as the front, and place the filler to match your bone shape.

Jawline Filler

Jawline filler adds shape and structure to the jaw with a dermal filler. It can make the jaw look more defined, the lower face look more sculpted, and the profile look stronger in photos. The provider places the filler along the jaw in small amounts, checks the line from the front and the side, and stops when the shape looks right.

Microneedling

Microneedling uses very fine needles to make tiny points in the skin. Those points prompt the skin to make more collagen, which keeps skin firm and smooth. Over time, texture, fine lines, small scars, and uneven tone may look better. The skin may look pink for a short while after, and your provider will give you care steps for home. A set of visits often works best.

Microneedling With Exosomes

Microneedling with exosomes adds tiny cell signals to the microneedling treatment. Those signals help the skin heal and make collagen, so the result can be stronger than microneedling alone. The skin may also calm down faster after the visit. People who want smoother skin, a brighter tone, and a short recovery often ask about this version.

Hair Restoration Microneedling

Hair restoration microneedling treats the scalp, not the face. Fine needles make tiny points on the scalp, which can bring more blood to the area and wake up the scalp's own repair work. Over a course of visits, hair may look thicker and fuller, though the change is slow. Your provider will look at your scalp and set a plan that fits your needs.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does Botox cost?

The cost depends on the spots treated and the amount used, so it is different for each person. A free consultation gives you a clear plan and a cost for that plan, with no surprise extras.

Will people know I had work done?

Most people will not, if the change is small and natural. Friends may say you look rested or bright, without knowing what changed. That is the goal of a light, well placed treatment.

Can I exercise after Botox?

Your provider will give you aftercare steps, and it is best to follow them for the first day. Ask the team if you have a workout, a game, or a long day planned, so you know what to do and what to wait on.

What is the best age to start Botox?

There is no set age. Some people start when lines begin to stay, and some start later. A free consultation lets your provider look at your skin and talk about whether it is a good time for you.

How long will my filler hold up?

Filler often lasts for months, but the exact time varies from person to person. Your body, the type of filler, and how much was used all play a part, and your provider can talk about timing at your visit.

Is microneedling safe for dark skin?

Your provider will check your skin type and tone at your free consultation before they suggest microneedling. That check helps them pick the right plan and the right care steps for you.

Do I need to stop skin care before a peel?

Your provider will tell you what to use and what to skip in the days before a peel. Bring the products you use at home to your consultation, so the team can give you clear advice.
